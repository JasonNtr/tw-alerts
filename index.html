Make it sound like an alarm

Alerts get their own notification category on Android, separate from Discord, so they can have their own sound:

  1. In Discord, type phone test in the control channel.
  2. When the alert arrives, press and hold it, then open its Settings.
  3. Pick an alarm sound and vibration, set importance to Urgent, and allow it to override Do Not Disturb.
  4. Tap Got it on the alert (or swipe it away) to stop it repeating. Left alone, it comes back every minute.
